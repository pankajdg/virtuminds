# Strategic Guidance Hub

I am creating an advisory website, I have already started working on it , I have attached what I have so far. I want to add 3 tabs to a floating navigation bar, one for services, one for about, and a schedule meeting button. This is the general structure and pages I want for the website, feel free to make changes based on what works best but try to stay true to all of the components, while overall keeping a very professional feel:Recommended page structure (single homepage first)

Hero – Full-width (photo or very restrained abstract), overlay text, two buttons.

Trust strip – “Experience Across Fortune 100 Enterprises” + logos (Cisco · Intuit · GE · HP · DirecTV · Wachovia · AOL · Constellation Energy).

About – Side-by-side: large professional photo + three short text blocks.

Track Record – 4 large photo cards (headline + result number only).

Services – Simple 6-item grid with one-line descriptions.

CTA Band – Full-width navy: “Focused guidance on your cyber risk decision” + Calendly button.

Footer – Email, LinkedIn, copyright.
